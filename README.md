# ML-and-Algebraic-Structures

This is the application of my Master thesis:

  1- Learning finite groups from the Latin Squares dataset.
  
  2- Identify if a finite group is simple or not just from looking at the Cayley table.
  
  3- Recognize isomorphism between finite groups.
  
  4- Recognize ring structure by testing with rings that has never seen by the machine.
  
  5- Classify the Dessin d'enfants by extension degree over the field of rational number.
